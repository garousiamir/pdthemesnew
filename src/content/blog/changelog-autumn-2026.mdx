The autumn releases are mostly about compatibility and polish. Each product's full changelog is on its product page; this is the short version.

## Aurum 1.4

- New full-bleed "story" product layout.
- Four campaign patterns for seasonal landing pages.
- Template parity with WooCommerce 9.x block templates.
- Fix: mini cart focus trap on Safari 18.

## Kontor 2.1

- New "Clay" style variation.
- Careers block can read a JSON feed.
- Fix: sticky header behaviour on iOS Safari.

## Formwerk 3.2

- Phone field with country formats.
- Retention policies per form (automatic deletion after a set period).
- Pro: step resume after session expiry.

## Tempo 1.6

- White-label change reports on the Agency tier.
- Delay-until-interaction script policy.

## Next

Gazette 1.1 (newsletter blocks for two more providers) and Gridwerk 2.1 (query-loop presets for WooCommerce) are in testing. Updates arrive in your WordPress admin as usual.
