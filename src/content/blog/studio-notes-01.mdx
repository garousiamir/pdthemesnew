A few people have asked why PDThemes sells directly rather than through a marketplace. The short answer is that it lets us keep three promises.

**Prices stay honest.** Without marketplace commission, the price on the product page is the price. Promotions, when they exist, are real and time-limited and configured in the checkout, not painted on.

**Support stays close.** A support request from a direct customer reaches the developer the same day. Marketplace support systems add a layer that, in our experience, mostly adds delay.

**Updates stay simple.** Through Freemius, updates arrive in your WordPress admin. No downloading a new zip from a marketplace account every few weeks.

We did consider building our own licensing system. We decided against it for the same reason we recommend you do not build your own form plugin: it is a product in itself, with its own tax rules, fraud rules and edge cases. Freemius does that work, and does it well.

Free products go to WordPress.org, where free products belong. Everything else is here.
