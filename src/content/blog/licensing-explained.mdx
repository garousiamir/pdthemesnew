Two questions arrive almost every week: "If the theme is GPL, why do I need a license?" and "What happens when the license expires?" Here are the straight answers.

## The software is GPL

Every PDThemes theme and plugin is released under the GNU General Public License. That means you can use it on as many sites as you like, modify it, and redistribute it under the same terms. We do not use license checks to switch off features or lock you out of your own site.

## The license is for services

What a license actually buys is **access to services** around the software:

- **Updates**, delivered to your WordPress admin through Freemius.
- **Support**, from the engineers who built the product.
- **Downloads** of every version from the customer portal.

The activation count on a license (1, 5 or unlimited sites) limits how many sites receive those services. It does not limit where you may run the code.

## When a license expires

The product keeps working. Nothing is disabled. You simply stop receiving updates and support until you renew. We think this is the only honest way to sell GPL software.

## Why Freemius

We use Freemius because it handles the parts of licensing we would otherwise get wrong: VAT across the EU, invoices, renewals, refunds, and a customer portal that is not another password to lose. It also means your payment details never touch our servers.

## In one paragraph

You are buying a year of updates and support for a set number of sites, for software you are free to keep and use forever. If that still leaves a question, the [license page](/license) has the specifics and [support](/support) is a message away.
